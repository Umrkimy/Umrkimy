## 🚀 Projects

### 🌙 [Venus](https://github.com/Umrkimy/Venus)
My own voice assistant that lives on my PC. I say "Hey Venus" and she opens my apps, controls my lights, reads my calendar and email, remembers things and talks back, with a 3D character on the web page. PC actions need my approval first.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Next.js` `TypeScript` `three.js` `Docker` `GitHub Actions`

### 🍓 [Bahulu Berry Cameron](https://github.com/Umrkimy/bahulu-berry-cameron-ai-assistant)
Operations platform for a real bakery in Cameron Highlands: storefront in English and Malay, staff and owner dashboard, inventory, orders and reports. The AI assistant reads live business data and proposes actions, and the owner confirms every change.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `Next.js` `Stripe (test)` `Docker` · 🚧 in development

### 🚩 [RedFlag](https://github.com/Umrkimy/RedFlag)
My final year project with a teammate: a web game that teaches students to spot scams, with AI characters and answers grounded in official scam advisories (RAG). I'm on backend, AI, security and DevOps.

`FastAPI` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL + pgvector` `OpenAI` · 🚧 in development

### 🏠 [Homelab](https://github.com/Umrkimy/Homelab-Umar)
An old laptop with a broken hinge turned into my home server: photo backup, network-wide ad blocking, uptime alerts on Telegram, all in Docker.

`Debian` `Docker` `Nginx Proxy Manager`
